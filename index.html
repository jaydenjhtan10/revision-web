Biology AQA

[4 marks]

Q1: Compare the structure of a red blood cell with the structure of a plant cell.

Reveal Answer
  • Red blood cell has no nucleus, plant cell does.
  • Red blood cell has no cell wall, plant cell does.
  • Red blood cell contains haemoglobin, plant cell does not.
  • Red blood cell is a biconcave disc, plant cells are often rectangular/fixed shape.
  • Plant cells contain chloroplasts and a permanent vacuole, red blood cells do not.
[3 marks]

Q2: Explain the 'lock and key' theory of enzyme action.

Reveal Answer

The enzyme has a specific active site (1). Only a substrate with a complementary shape can fit into the active site (2). This forms an enzyme-substrate complex, allowing the reaction to occur (3).

Chemistry AQA

[4 marks]

Q1: Explain why graphite conducts electricity but diamond does not. Both are forms of carbon.

Reveal Answer

In graphite, each carbon atom forms only 3 covalent bonds, leaving one delocalised electron per atom (2). These delocalised electrons are free to move and carry charge through the structure (1). In diamond, all 4 outer electrons are used in covalent bonding, so there are no delocalised electrons to carry charge (1).

Physics AQA

[3 marks]

Q1: A car has a mass of 1200 kg and is traveling at 20 m/s. Calculate its kinetic energy.

Reveal Answer

Equation: KE = 0.5 × m × v²

KE = 0.5 × 1200 × (20²)

KE = 0.5 × 1200 × 400 = 240,000 J (or 240 kJ)

Computer Science OCR

[4 marks]

Q1: Describe the difference between RAM and ROM.

Reveal Answer
  • RAM is volatile (loses data when power is off), ROM is non-volatile.
  • RAM is read and write, ROM is read-only.
  • RAM stores currently running programs and data, ROM stores the boot-up instructions (BIOS).
